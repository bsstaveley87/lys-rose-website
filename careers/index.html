FORT LAUDERDALE

Production Assistant (Part-Time)

About us

Lys Rose Baby Spa is a mobile luxury baby spa in Fort Lauderdale. We host calm, guided rituals for parents and their babies (a floral milk bath, infant massage and guided baby face care), guided by a caring instructor while each parent cares for their own baby.

What you'll do

  • Set up and break down each session: mats, tubs, towels and robes, refreshment table, photo corner. Leave every space exactly as we found it.
  • Welcome families warmly, check them in and help them settle.
  • Run food and beverage service for parents.
  • Keep the space spotless: wipe down the play area and tubs between families and at breakdown, keep floors dry, and follow our hygiene standards.
  • Support our session instructor so the session runs smoothly and on time.
  • Between sessions, on flexible hours: launder and prepare linens, clean and log reusable tools, restock supplies, and run errands and pickups.
  • You never hold or handle babies. Parents do all the baby care.

What we're looking for

  • Reliable and on time, with a calm, warm and discreet presence around babies and new or nursing parents.
  • Careful with food handling and hygiene.
  • Comfortable lifting and carrying supplies (up to about 30 lb) and on your feet for 3–4 hours.
  • A car, a valid driver's license and current auto insurance.
  • Willing to complete a fingerprint background check (paid by us).

Nice to have

  • Event, hospitality, catering or production assistant experience.
  • Experience with new parents or infants.
  • Spa, hospitality or luxury guest-service experience.

Schedule

Part-time and flexible. Weekday or weekend sessions (3–4 hours each) when scheduled, plus flexible hours for prep and errands that you fit around your week.

Pay

$20/hour, plus mileage reimbursement.
