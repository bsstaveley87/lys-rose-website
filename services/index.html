Services

SERVICES

The session

A calm time for you and your baby, in an intimate small group.

INCLUDED

Each session includes

i. Guided infant massage

Infant massage, with a caring expert in babies and new parents.

ii. A warm floral milk bath

A warm floral milk bath, prepared for you.

iii. Guided baby face care

Baby face care with a fragrance-free baby face cream.

iv. A baby robe set

A soft baby robe set for a photo moment. A take-home baby robe is available to add.

v. One baby per parent

Each parent cares for one baby.

vi. Two hours together

Each session lasts 2 hours.

vii. Light refreshments

Light refreshments are included.

THE DETAILS

Who, how and where

WHO IT'S FOR

Parents and their babies under 12 months. Sessions are grouped by baby's age: 0–3, 3–6, 6–9 and 9–12 months.

WHO LEADS IT

Every Lys Rose session is led by a caring professional from the world of babies and new parents, whose backgrounds may include pediatric or pelvic floor physical therapy, doula work, midwifery or infant massage instruction. All are CPR certified and chosen for their calm, nurturing way with families. They lead each step, and you share it hand in hand with your baby.

HOW IT WORKS

Pop-up sessions for a small group of parents in Fort Lauderdale. At home on request.

AT HOME

Prefer to stay home? We also come to you, on request.

WHERE WE ARE

Fort Lauderdale, with in-home sessions on request.

WHAT TO BRING

Just your baby, a couple of diapers, a change of clothes and anything you need for feeding (bottle, formula or a nursing cover). We take care of the rest: towels, wipes and everything used during the session.
