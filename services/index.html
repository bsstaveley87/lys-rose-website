Services

SERVICES

The session

A calm, guided time for you and your baby, shared with a small group of other parents. You do every part yourself, with your own baby, while our team demonstrates and guides.

INCLUDED

Each session includes

i. Guided infant massage

You'll learn infant massage step by step, guided by an experienced practitioner, as you massage your own baby.

ii. A warm floral milk bath

We prepare a warm floral milk bath, and you'll learn to bathe your baby in it, with our guidance.

iii. Guided baby face care

Guided baby face care. With our instructor guiding you, you'll gently moisturize your baby's face with a fragrance-free baby face cream. You do it yourself; our staff don't perform any skin treatments.

iv. Robes and hooded towels

Soft robes in 0–6M and 6–9M sizes, and hooded towels for babies 9 months and up.

v. One baby per parent

Each parent cares for one baby, with gentle guidance throughout.

vi. Two hours together

Each session lasts 2 hours.

vii. Light refreshments

Light refreshments are included.

THE DETAILS

Who, how and where

WHO IT'S FOR

Parents and their babies under 12 months. Sessions are grouped by baby's age: 0–3, 3–6, 6–9 and 9–12 months.

WHO LEADS IT

Every Lys Rose session is led by a caring professional from the world of babies and new parents, whose backgrounds may include pediatric or pelvic floor physical therapy, doula work, midwifery or infant massage instruction. All are CPR certified and chosen for their calm, nurturing way with families. They lead each step, and you share it hand in hand with your baby.

HOW IT WORKS

Pop-up, retreat-style sessions for a small group of parents, in a welcoming local venue. You do each step with your own baby while we demonstrate and guide.

AT HOME

Prefer to stay home? We also come to you, on request.

WHERE WE ARE

Fort Lauderdale, with in-home sessions on request.

WHAT TO BRING

Diapers, a change of clothes, and a bottle or feeding supplies. Robes and hooded towels are provided.
