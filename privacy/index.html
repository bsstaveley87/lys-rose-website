What we collect

Information you give us (waitlist form):

  • Parent or guardian name and email address (required).
  • Optional details: your baby's age range (or that you're expecting), your baby's name, your area and ZIP code, your preferred dates, your mobile phone number, what interests you, and how you heard about us.
  • Anything you write to us by email or direct message.
  • [TO CONFIRM – add only if built:] your text-message choice (whether you checked the texting box), with the date and time and the wording you agreed to.
  • [TO CONFIRM – add when booking opens:] booking details, and any allergy or skin-sensitivity information you choose to share so we can prepare your session safely.

Information you give us when you book [DRAFT, N51, Christy Nov 4, 2026, pending attorney review]:

  • Your baby's birth date. We collect it at booking to confirm your baby meets our age rule (at least 4 weeks old and no older than 12 months on the session date), to plan age-appropriate sessions, and to understand our customers (business analysis, looked at in aggregate). We keep it, together with your booking record, for 3 years after your family's last booking, store it securely with access limited to Lys Rose, and never sell it. After that, we delete the full birth date and keep only your baby's age in months at each session, for business analysis. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Health confirmations. The booking form asks you to tick boxes confirming, for example, that in the 24 hours before the session your baby has had no fever, vomiting or diarrhea. We store only whether each box was ticked (yes/no). We don't collect any health details.
  • IP address and browser details when you sign your waiver
  • If you give us an emergency contact, we collect their name, phone number and relationship to you. We use these only to reach that person if there's an emergency during your session, and we keep them with your signed waiver.

We don't ask for your baby's birth date or about health on the waitlist. Please share only what you're comfortable with.

How we use your information

  • To keep you on our waitlist and tell you when sessions open.
  • To plan sessions in areas and at times that suit the families who've signed up, and to suggest the right session for your baby's age.
  • To reply to your questions and requests.
  • To send you Lys Rose news by email. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To send text messages, only if you've opted in (see "Text messages").
  • [When booking opens:] to take bookings and payments, send confirmations and reminders, and prepare your session safely.
  • To keep our site and form secure, prevent spam and keep business records the law requires.

We don't sell your personal information

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don't share it with others for their own marketing or advertising.

Text messages

If you check the optional texting box, we'll send recurring text messages to the mobile number you gave us, such as waitlist updates, booking reminders and offers. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to texts is never required to join the waitlist or to buy anything. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also opt out by emailing hello@lysrosebabyspa.com. See our SMS Terms [link].

Mobile numbers and text-message opt-in information are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share your number only with our texting provider, so it can deliver our messages to you.

Cookies and similar technologies

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Our analytics tool (Cloudflare Web Analytics) doesn't use cookies. Our providers may use strictly necessary technologies to keep the site secure and working. [TO CONFIRM: if the site moves to Squarespace, or a booking/payment tool is added, those providers may set their own functional or analytics cookies; list them here.] You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work. Because we don't track you across other websites, we don't change our practices in response to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

Information about babies comes from parents

Our website is meant for parents and guardians. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Any information about your baby (such as name or age range) comes from you, as the parent or guardian, and we use it only for the purposes in this policy. If you think a child has sent us information directly, email us and we'll delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information for up to [24 – TO CONFIRM] months after our last contact with you, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. We keep records of text-message consent and opt-outs for [4 – TO CONFIRM] years so we can show we respected your choices. We limit access to the people who need it, use providers with industry-standard security, and protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-step sign-in. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but if a security breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as Florida law requires.
