Privacy

Effective [date]. Lys Rose LLC ("Lys Rose," "we," "us"), Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

This policy explains what we collect when you join our waitlist or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, you give us:

  • your name (parent or guardian), email and phone number
  • your area and zip code
  • your baby's name and age (or that you're expecting)
  • your preferred dates and interests, and how you heard about us

Please share only what you're comfortable with. We don't ask about allergies or health on the waitlist. When you book a session, our booking form asks about any allergies for you or your baby, so we can prepare safely. We use that information only for your session and share it only with the staff running it. We don't knowingly collect information from children themselves. Details about your baby come only from you, as their parent or guardian.

How we use it

  • to contact you about session dates, openings and your booking
  • to plan sessions near you and at times that work for you
  • once you book, to keep you and your baby safe, for example by leaving out an ingredient you're allergic to
  • to send occasional Lys Rose news by email, if you've agreed to it. You can unsubscribe any time using the link in every email.

Text messages

If you check the texting box, we'll send recurring automated marketing texts to the number you gave us: a welcome text, a text when booking opens (with the booking link), and reminders while a session is still open for booking. Message frequency varies, usually no more than one text a day and a few a week while booking is open. We send texts only between 9 AM and 7 PM your local time. Consent isn't required to join the waitlist or to buy anything. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. You can also opt out by emailing us. We never share or sell your mobile number or texting consent to anyone else for their marketing.

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising by others. We share it only with:

  • service providers that help us run the waitlist and bookings (our form provider Tally, our email and file storage tools, and our payment processor once booking opens), and only to provide those services
  • our session staff, who receive only what they need for your session (for example, your first name and, once you've booked, any allergies you told us about)
  • authorities, if the law requires it

How we protect it

We limit who can see your information and use accounts protected by passwords and two-step sign-in. If a data breach ever affects you, we'll tell you as Florida law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information for up to [24] months after your last contact with us, then delete it. Booking and payment records are kept longer when tax or business law requires it.

Your choices

Email hello@lysrosebabyspa.com at any time to see, correct or delete your information, or to leave the waitlist. We'll respond within [30] days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the update here with a new effective date.
