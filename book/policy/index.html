Welcome

Thank you for choosing Lys Rose Baby Spa. Our sessions are small, gentle gatherings for parents and their babies (babies at least 4 weeks old and no older than 12 months on the session date), each led by a caring professional from the world of babies and new parents. We keep groups small so every parent and baby gets real attention, which means each booked place matters. This policy explains how booking, payment, changes and refunds work.

Seller: Lys Rose LLC, doing business as Lys Rose Baby Spa.

About our sessions

  • Format: an approximately 2-hour group session. Each parent comes with their own baby, at least 4 weeks old and no older than 12 months on the session date.
  • The meeting place is confirmed in your booking confirmation. In-home sessions are available on request.
  • Each session is led by a caring professional from the world of babies and new parents. Backgrounds may include pediatric or pelvic floor physical therapy, doula work, midwifery or infant massage instruction. All are CPR certified.
  • Lys Rose sessions are educational. They are not medical care, physical therapy, midwifery or massage therapy.
  • Sessions run with a minimum group size. If it isn't reached, we'll offer a full refund or a free reschedule.
  • The price is the amount shown when you book, per parent and baby.

Booking and payment

Your place is held once payment is received and you get a confirmation by email.

You pay the full in one payment at booking. of it is a nonrefundable deposit that holds your spot and counts toward the session. It isn't a separate charge. The deposit is never refunded, but it can go toward a new session for your family booked within 60 days. It can't be transferred to another family, and the baby must still meet the age rule (at least 4 weeks old and no older than 12 months) on the new date. The cancellation refunds below apply only to the balance.

We decide whether each session goes ahead 5 days before it, and we don't take payment for a date until our insurance is bound. If a session doesn't go ahead, everyone booked is offered a full refund or a free reschedule.

Regular booking stays open until about 48 hours before the session. The personalized embroidered robe add-on closes 14 days before the session, so robes can be embroidered. A personalized robe isn't refunded once it has been sent for embroidery.

Every parent attending must sign the Lys Rose waiver and intake form before attending.

Your baby must be at least 4 weeks old and no older than 12 months on the day of your session. The umbilical cord stump must have fallen off and the area must be fully healed, and your baby's skin must have no breaks. If your baby has been circumcised, that site must also be fully healed. We ask for your baby's date of birth and check it against the session date. The waiver asks you to confirm that you've talked with your pediatrician about the products and activities used in the session, and that you can skip any part at any time.

You can skip any part of the session at any time. Skipping a part doesn't change the session price, so there is no refund or discount for it. Please tell us at least 48 hours before your session about any allergy or sensitivity.

If you need to cancel

Your payment includes a nonrefundable deposit. It isn't refunded, but it can go toward a new session for your family booked within 60 days, as long as your baby still meets the age rule on the new date. It can't be transferred to another family. These tiers apply only to the balance:

  • 7 or more days before the session: the balance is refunded.
  • Less than 7 days, and 48 hours or more before: is refunded.
  • Within 48 hours: no refund. You may transfer your place to another parent up to 24 hours before.

These tiers don't apply when your baby is unwell or turned away, or when Lys Rose cancels. If Lys Rose cancels, you get a full refund, including the deposit.

Add-ons follow the same timing as the balance, and the sales tax on them is refunded too. A personalized robe isn't refunded once it has been sent for embroidery. If Lys Rose cancels, add-ons are refunded in full. If your baby is unwell or turned away, add-ons move with the booking, or into store credit if no date works.

To cancel, reply to your booking confirmation or write to hello@lysrosebabyspa.com. The time we receive your message is your cancellation time. Please cancel in writing so there is a clear record.

Credits are for the person who booked and can be used for any Lys Rose session. Credits don't expire, and no fees are charged on them. Credits have no cash value, except where this policy offers a refund.

If your baby is unwell

Please stay home if you or your baby are unwell, and tell us as soon as you can, even on the morning of the session. We'll offer a free reschedule to a Lys Rose session within 60 days first. If no new date works, we'll offer store credit. There is no cash refund for illness, and the cancellation tiers don't apply. Your whole payment moves to the new date or into credit, the deposit included. No doctor's note is needed.

Please stay home if, in the 24 hours before the session, your baby has had a fever, vomiting or diarrhea, or has green nasal mucus, or if you or your baby have any other contagious illness.

If your baby seems unwell at check-in, the instructor may ask you not to join. You'll be offered a free reschedule within 60 days, or store credit if no new date works, not a cash refund.

Turned away at check-in

If your baby is turned away at check-in because your baby seems unwell, or doesn't meet the eligibility rules, we'll offer a free reschedule within 60 days, or store credit if no new date works. There is no cash refund. Your whole payment moves, the deposit included. A reschedule must be to a date when your baby still meets the age rule. If no qualifying date works, you'll get store credit.

Before you pay, you confirm the age and cord rules, that there's no refund or discount for skipping any part of the session, and that your baby can only join if, in the 24 hours before the session, your baby has had no fever, vomiting or diarrhea, has no green nasal mucus, has no other signs of illness (such as a rash or cough), and has no breaks in the skin. You also confirm that you understand a baby who seems unwell at check-in may be asked not to join, with a reschedule or store credit rather than a cash refund, and that you'll contact Lys Rose before the session if your baby becomes unwell. We store those answers only as yes or no, with the time you confirmed them. We don't collect health details.

Late arrival

  • Up to 15 minutes late: come in quietly and we will help you settle.
  • More than 30 minutes late: we will do our best to include you, but we may not be able to repeat activities already covered, and the session still finishes at the scheduled time.
  • Late arrival does not change the session price, and time missed is not refunded.
  • If you are running late, please text us so we can save your spot.

No-shows

If you do not attend and do not contact us before the session starts, this is a no-show. There is no refund or credit for a no-show. If you could not reach us because your baby or you were unwell, contact us within 24 hours after the session and we will treat it as an illness: a free reschedule or store credit.

Transferring your place

You may give or sell your place to another parent. Transfers are free up to 24 hours before the session. The new parent's baby must meet the age rule and the healed-skin rules. Send us the new parent's name, email and phone number. The new parent must sign their own waiver and intake form before the session. Waivers cannot be transferred. After a transfer, the new parent receives the same illness reschedule. Any payment between you and the other parent is between the two of you. A later refund for that booking goes to the original purchaser.

If Lys Rose cancels

If we cancel or need to move a session for any reason, you can choose a full refund or a free reschedule. That includes the deposit. Reasons include the minimum group size not being reached, illness of the lead professional if we cannot arrange another caring professional, the meeting place becoming unavailable or unsafe, weather that makes travel unsafe, or insurance or required paperwork not being ready. We will contact you by email as soon as we know. If a session moves to a new date that doesn't work for you, you can still choose a full refund. If the lead professional changes but the session still runs with another caring professional from the world of babies and new parents, the session goes ahead as booked.

Gift purchases

The person buying is the purchaser. The parent attending is the recipient. The recipient books the date and must sign their own waiver. Gift cancellations follow the same rules as any booking, from the recipient's session date. The illness reschedule applies. Gift purchases are not refunded for cash, except where this policy offers a refund or the law requires one. If Lys Rose cancels a session paid with a gift, the value stays on the gift for another session. Any refund we give goes back to the purchaser. Gifts don't expire, and no fees are charged after purchase.

How refunds are paid

Refunds made within 1 year of your payment go back to the original payment method. After 1 year, or if that account has been closed, we will refund you another way, such as a check. We aim to process an approved refund within 2 business days. Once processed, it usually takes 4 to 14 business days to arrive, depending on your bank. You will receive an email when the refund is issued. Credits don't expire. If something has gone wrong with a payment, contact us first. This doesn't affect your rights with your card issuer.

In-home sessions

In-home sessions are quoted individually. The price, travel area and any travel amount are confirmed in writing before payment. A deposit, shown as a dollar amount on your quote, holds the date, and the balance is due 7 days before. If the balance isn't paid by then, the date is released and the deposit becomes credit toward a future session. Credits don't expire. If you cancel 14 or more days before, you receive a full refund of what you have paid. If you cancel less than 14 days and at least 48 hours before, you receive credit toward a future session. Within 48 hours, there is no refund or credit. If your baby is unwell, the illness terms apply. If we cancel, you choose a full refund or a free reschedule. The host provides a safe, clean, warm space. Every parent signs the waiver before attending.

Waiver

Every adult attending (the booking parent and any second adult) must sign the Lys Rose waiver before taking part. After you book, we'll email each adult a link to read and sign it electronically. You can read the waiver before you book: Read the waiver.

  • Please sign at least 48 hours before your session. If you haven't signed by then, you can sign at check-in, before the session starts.
  • If an adult hasn't signed by the start of the session, that adult can't take part. If the booking parent doesn't sign, the booking is treated as a cancellation inside 48 hours (no refund), the same as not attending. A second adult who doesn't sign can't join, and the second-adult add-on is not refunded.
  • If you read the waiver after booking and decide not to sign, tell us before the session. We'll treat it as a cancellation on the day you tell us, under Section 4 (the $100 deposit is nonrefundable).
  • If you receive a transferred place, the new parent signs their own waiver (see Section 8). Waivers can't be transferred.

The waiver is a separate document. This booking policy does not replace it, and nothing in this policy changes what the waiver says.

The waiver includes a parent confirmation of the baby's minimum age, the healed cord, a fully healed circumcision site if applicable, health on the day and that the parent has talked with their pediatrician about the products and activities used in the session (Clause A), and the product list, the opt-out rule (no refund, credit or discount, and a declined paid upgrade is not charged) and the parent's duty to disclose allergies 48 hours ahead (Clause B).

Health, safety and conduct

Please share relevant health information on the intake form. The person leading the session may adjust or pause any activity for comfort or safety. We may ask anyone whose behaviour is unsafe or disruptive to leave.

Products

Products bought separately, such as our Floral Milk Soak, are not covered by the session terms. Those sales are final. If a product arrives damaged, is defective, or isn't what you ordered, message us within 7 days with a photo and we'll replace it or, if we can't, refund it.

Complimentary events

Complimentary, invite-only events are free. No payment is taken and these refund terms don't apply. If your plans change, tell us so we can offer the place to someone else.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version shown when you booked applies to your booking.
