OUR STORY

Lily Rose

Hi, I'm Christy Zemour, founder of Lys Rose Baby Spa. Lys Rose is named for my daughter, and its French name is an ode to my heritage: I'm half French and half Swedish. Lys is French for lily.

The idea came from the small-group baby spa sessions popular in Europe, where parents gather with their babies for a calm, guided time together. Lys Rose brings that feeling to South Florida: guided, hands-on time with your baby, where you'll learn each step yourself.

We hold small group sessions in a welcoming local venue in Fort Lauderdale. Prefer to stay home? We can come to you, on request.

The Lys Rose way

Gentle, calm and unhurried. Every detail, from the soft robes to the warm floral milk bath, is chosen to give parents and babies a quiet moment together.

Lys Rose sessions are educational and do not provide medical, physical therapy, midwifery or massage therapy services.

Every Lys Rose session is led by a caring professional from the world of babies and new parents, whose backgrounds may include pediatric or pelvic floor physical therapy, doula work, midwifery or infant massage instruction. All are CPR certified and chosen for their calm, nurturing way with families. They lead each step, and you share it hand in hand with your baby.
