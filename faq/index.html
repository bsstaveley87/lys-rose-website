FAQ

QUESTIONS

Good to know

Who are the sessions for?+

Parents and their babies under 12 months. Each parent cares for one baby.

Are sessions grouped by baby's age?+

Yes. Sessions are split by baby's age: 0–3 months, 3–6 months, 6–9 months and 9–12 months.

Who leads the session?+

Every Lys Rose session is guided by a caring professional from the world of babies and new parents, whose backgrounds may include pediatric or pelvic floor physical therapy, doula work, midwifery or infant massage instruction. All are CPR certified and chosen for their calm, nurturing way with families. They guide each step, and you share it hand in hand with your baby.

Where do sessions take place?+

In a welcoming local venue in Fort Lauderdale. While a session is open for booking, you'll find the address on our Services page.

Can you come to us instead?+

Yes. Prefer to stay home? We also come to you, on request.

How many parents are in a group?+

It's a small group session, so there's time for every parent and baby.

What's included in a session?+

A guided infant massage, a warm floral milk bath and guided baby face care, all done by you with your own baby while we demonstrate and guide, plus light refreshments. A robe and hooded towels to wear during your session, and a soft baby brush (cleaned and sanitized after every use), are provided. A fresh robe set to take home is available as an optional add-on.

How long is a session?+

2 hours.

What should I bring?+

Diapers, a change of clothes, and a bottle or feeding supplies. A robe (0–6M and 6–9M) and hooded towels (9M and up) are provided to wear during your session, and light refreshments are included.

What's in the floral milk bath?+

Our milk bath is a simple blend of coconut milk powder, colloidal oatmeal and dried rose petals. We can also make it with whole cow's milk.

What if my baby or I have an allergy or sensitive skin?+

When you book, please tell us about any allergies or sensitivities, anything in the products we use during your session you'd like us to avoid, and whether your baby has a skin condition such as a rash, eczema or broken skin. We'll set up your bath without that ingredient, and on the day we can always offer a plain warm-water bath. If you're unsure about anything, please check with your pediatrician before your session.

Can I bring twins, or come with a partner?+

Twins are welcome. Please come with a second adult, such as a partner, grandparent or aunt, so each baby has their own grown-up, and book a separate place for each twin. A second parent or family member can also join one parent-and-baby place for an add-on fee; we'll share the details when sessions open.

Is this a medical or therapy service?+

No. Lys Rose sessions are educational and do not provide medical, physical therapy, midwifery or massage therapy services.
