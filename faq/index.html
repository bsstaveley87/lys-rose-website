FAQ

QUESTIONS

Good to know

Who are the sessions for?+

Parents and their babies under 12 months. Each parent cares for one baby.

Are sessions grouped by baby's age?+

Yes. Sessions are split by baby's age: 0–3 months, 3–6 months, 6–9 months and 9–12 months.

Who leads the session?+

Every Lys Rose session is led by a caring professional from the world of babies and new parents, whose backgrounds may include pediatric or pelvic floor physical therapy, doula work, midwifery or infant massage instruction. All are CPR certified and chosen for their calm, nurturing way with families. They lead each step, and you share it hand in hand with your baby.

Where do sessions take place?+

In Fort Lauderdale. While a session is open for booking, you'll find the address on our Services page.

Can you come to us instead?+

Yes. Prefer to stay home? We also come to you, on request.

How many parents are in a group?+

It's a small group session, so there's time for every parent and baby.

What's included in my session?+

A 2-hour session for you and your baby, with a floral milk bath, baby massage and face care, a soft baby robe set, refreshments, light bites and fresh florals. We handle the laundry and cleanup, so you can simply enjoy. Petit cadeau: a little gift.

See the full list in What's included.

How long is a session?+

2 hours.

What should I bring?+

Just your baby, a couple of diapers, a change of clothes and anything you need for feeding (bottle, formula or a nursing cover). We take care of the rest: towels, wipes and everything used during the session.

What's in the floral milk bath?+

Our milk bath is a simple blend of coconut milk powder, colloidal oatmeal and dried rose petals. We can also make it with whole cow's milk.

What if my baby or I have an allergy or sensitive skin?+

Before your session, we'll share the full ingredient list for the milk soak, the grapeseed massage oil and the baby face cream. Please tell us at least 48 hours ahead about any allergies or sensitivities, including to coconut, oats, milk, nuts, grapes, soy, avocado, flowers or pollen, or anything in these products, and whether your baby has sensitive skin or a skin condition. We'll prepare your baby's bath without that ingredient, and on the day we can always switch to a plain warm-water bath. If you're not sure, check with your pediatrician before your session.

Can I bring twins, or come with a partner?+

Twins are welcome. Please come with a second adult, such as a partner, grandparent or aunt, so each baby has their own grown-up, and book a separate place for each twin. A second parent or family member can also join one parent-and-baby place for an add-on fee; we'll share the details when sessions open.

Is this a medical or therapy service?+

No. Lys Rose sessions are educational and do not provide medical, physical therapy, midwifery or massage therapy services.
